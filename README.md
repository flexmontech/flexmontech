# FLEXMON LLC

**Technology + AI solutions for small and medium-sized businesses.**

FLEXMON LLC is an Illinois-based technology and consulting business delivering practical digital solutions, AI-enabled applications, workflow automation, healthcare IT support and web development.

## What we do

- **AI and automation** — AI-enabled tools, agents and workflow automation
- **Healthcare IT** — healthcare informatics, digital operations and technology delivery
- **Web design and development** — business websites, web applications and digital experiences
- **Business applications** — practical tools for lead generation, customer engagement and operations
- **Branding and digital marketing** — logos, social content and promotional assets

## Selected websites and products

### [FLEXMON](https://flexmon.tech)
The FLEXMON website introduces the company's technology, AI and digital services, with a website chatbot to help visitors find information and understand available support. It reduces the friction of initial enquiries by giving visitors another way to get answers and explore services.

### [AI FlexConnect](https://aiflexconnect.lovable.app)
AI FlexConnect is a web application concept designed to bring AI work opportunities, training platforms and learning resources into one discovery experience. It addresses the challenge of searching across scattered sources; the public version currently showcases the front-end experience with sample content.

### [OceanHQ](https://oceanhq.lovable.app) (formerly KinetiqTrade)
OceanHQ is an AI workspace for chat-based tasks, live search, lead generation, document retrieval and client searches. It brings conversational interaction, tool-assisted workflows and access to information into one workspace, reducing the need to switch between separate tools.
[View the live OceanHQ demo](https://oceanhq.lovable.app) · [View the OceanHQ project repository](https://github.com/flexmontech/kinetiqtrade)

### [Plutonium31 – Lead Generation](https://github.com/flexmontech/legeseb-phase-1-foundation)
Plutonium31 (formerly Legeseb) is a configurable lead-generation and lead-management application for organising prospects, campaigns, qualification and editable outreach drafts. It helps small businesses bring scattered prospect information and follow-up preparation into a more structured workflow. The GitHub repository retains its original Legeseb-based URL for continuity.
[View the live Plutonium31 demo](https://plutonium31.lovable.app)

### [TrustCare Sales Agent](https://github.com/flexmontech/trustcare)
TrustCare is an early-stage sales-support agent built with cto.new for a healthcare services organisation. It provides a lead-generation workspace intended to help organise enquiries and support follow-up, reducing manual enquiry administration while keeping decisions and external communications under human control.
[View the TrustCare lead-generation demo](https://a7e68b7db624acfc890b6b0ca3c3d222.cto.site/leads)

### [TABs Behavioral Health](https://www.tabsbehavioralhealthservices.com/)
FLEXMON delivered the TABs Behavioral Health website and supporting brand assets, including logo and social-media promotional materials. The website gives the organisation a clear digital presence where visitors can learn about its services and find a route to make contact.

### [iDrink by Zipoura](https://idrinkapp.com)
iDrink by Zipoura is a hospitality-focused digital product for customer engagement and business growth. FLEXMON led the web-front-end delivery and technical coordination of the mobile-app development, testing and app-store release, helping bring the product to market across web and mobile.
